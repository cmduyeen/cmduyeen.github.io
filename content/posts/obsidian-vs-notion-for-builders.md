If you are tired of slow cloud apps and rising monthly SaaS fees, choosing the right knowledge system comes down to one question: **do you want a hosted database (Notion) or local plain-text files you own forever (Obsidian)?**

## Quick Comparison: Obsidian vs Notion

| Feature | Obsidian (Local-First) | Notion (Cloud Workspace) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pricing (Personal)** | **100% Free** (no account needed) | Free tier with block/AI limits |
| **Data Ownership** | Plain `.md` files on your drive | Stored on Notion's cloud servers |
| **Offline Speed** | Instant (0ms local read) | Limited offline caching |
| **Team Collaboration** | Requires Sync / Git | Built-in real-time sharing |
| **Publishing to Web** | Export straight to [Hugo + GitHub](/tools/) | Notion Sites / paid custom domains |

## When Notion Wins
Choose **Notion** if you work in a non-technical team that needs shared Kanban boards, embedded databases, and real-time comments without touching Markdown files.

## When Obsidian Wins (Zero Subscription Fatigue)
Choose **Obsidian** if you want **speed, privacy, and zero lock-in**:
- Your notes never disappear if a startup shuts down or changes pricing.
- Every note is clean Markdown—ready to feed into AI CLI tools or publish directly as a static blog.
