Forgot your handheld scientific calculator at home? **[VN-580 Plus Online](https://cmduyeen.github.io/may-tinh-khoa-hoc-online/)** runs 100% in your browser (desktop or mobile, even offline) with a familiar 580VN-style layout tailored for Vietnamese students.

## Key Features

- **Exact & Decimal (`S⇔D`)**: Automatically recognizes fractions, common surds (`√2/2`, `√3/2`), and `π` multiples, plus mixed fractions (`a b/c`) and DMS (`° ' "`).
- **Calculus & Root Finding**: Numerical definite integrals (`∫dx`), derivatives (`d/dx`), summations (`Σ`), products (`Π`), `CALC`, and `SOLVE` (`SHIFT` + `CALC`).
- **Number Theory**: Combinations (`nCr`), permutations (`nPr`), `GCD`, `LCM`, and prime factorization (`FACT`).
- **Full Mode Suite (`MENU`)**:
  - `2:CMPLX` — Complex numbers (`a + bi`, `r∠θ`, `Arg`, `Conjg`)
  - `3:BASE-N` — `DEC / HEX / BIN / OCT` conversions and bitwise ops (`AND`, `OR`, `XOR`, `NOT`)
  - `4:MATRIX` & `5:VECTOR` — Matrix determinant/inverse/multiply, vector dot/cross/angle
  - `6:STAT` — 1-variable stats and 2-variable linear regression (`y = a + bx`, `r`)
  - `8:TABLE` — Generate `f(x)` and `g(x)` value tables
  - `9:EQUATION` & `A:INEQ` — Systems of 2–4 linear equations, degree 2–4 polynomials, and degree 2–3 inequalities
- **5 Switchable Skins**: Classic 580, Hacker Matrix, Kawaii Pink, Anime Neon, and Cozy Study.
