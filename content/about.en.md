I'm Dyn, based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. I enjoy reading, cooking, and building small, practical software tools.

This blog is where I document what I learn about automation, personal knowledge management (Obsidian), and designing simple systems that last. I believe in calm, minimalist software (*less is more*): plain-text Markdown, fast load times, zero-install workflows, and respect for user privacy.
